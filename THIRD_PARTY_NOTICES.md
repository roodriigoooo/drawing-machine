## Quick, Draw!

The [Quick, Draw! dataset](https://github.com/googlecreativelab/quickdraw-dataset)
is made available by Google, Inc. under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Credit also belongs to the people who contributed drawings.

Examples in figures and demo records include dataset-derived training
neighbours and held-out prefixes. Processing includes simplification,
coordinate conversion, rendering and, for continuations, model-generated
extensions. The original dataset is not bundled. These changes and model
outputs do not imply endorsement by Google or dataset contributors.
